A short course

RabbitMQ in a Laravel e-commerce app

Built for one job: walking into an interview at a company that runs a Laravel shop selling electronics, uses RabbitMQ behind it, and is experimenting with AI tools, and being able to explain how the pieces fit, why they were chosen, and where they break.

The path is sequential. Each lesson uses only ideas from the lessons before it, takes about 12 to 15 minutes, and ends with practice you answer from memory.
